About Mutag House

Mutag House is an end-to-end business solutions partner based in Centurion, Gauteng. We help organisations form, grow, and run — through business setup and compliance, marketing services, and technology services.

The three pillars can be engaged together or alone. Some clients come to us to register a company and stay CIPC-compliant. Others need a brand, a website, and campaigns. Others need custom software, cybersecurity, or the ICT that keeps an office connected. Many need a mix, and they want one partner instead of a scattered vendor list.

Where marketing and technology meet, we treat that as one MarTech practice: brands, campaigns, websites, and systems designed to work together rather than as disconnected projects.

We work with startups, SMEs, corporates, and institutions across South Africa. The work is practical, scoped to the outcome, and built to last beyond launch.

At our core, we are a business partner — not a single-service studio.

Our Approach

Understand the Business

What We Do

At Mutag House, the work sits in three equal pillars. You can engage one, or we coordinate all three:

  • Business Solutions: Company registration, CIPC beneficial ownership, business documentation, CRM implementation, and investor or sales decks — the setup that lets the company operate.
  • Marketing Services: Brand identity, print and giftings, corporate websites and landing pages, SEO, content, social, and paid campaigns.
  • Technology Services: Custom software, APIs, dashboards, automation, and e-commerce platforms — plus cybersecurity, managed IT, cloud, cabling, and scoped ICT projects.

Where marketing and technology meet, brands, campaigns, and systems are designed as one MarTech practice. Whether you are registering a company, launching a brand, or modernising infrastructure, we have the expertise to take the brief through to delivery.
