Who We Are

MUTAG HOUSE is a software development studio registered in Centurion, Gauteng, South Africa, that designs and builds custom web applications, backend systems, and automation platforms for startups, SMEs, corporates, and institutions. We architect, build, and scale enterprise-grade software solutions—from custom web applications and backend systems to authentication platforms, business intelligence dashboards, and API integrations.

We work with startups, SMEs, corporates, and institutions that need more than a website—they need systems that handle complex business logic, integrate with existing infrastructure, and scale with growth. Our approach combines clean architecture principles, modern development practices, and strategic thinking to deliver software solutions that are maintainable, secure, and built for the long term.
