Who We Are

Mutag House is an end-to-end business solutions partner registered in Centurion, Gauteng, South Africa. We help organisations form, grow, and run — through business setup and compliance, marketing services, and technology services. Where marketing and technology meet, we treat that as one MarTech practice: brands, campaigns, websites, and systems that work together.

We work with startups, SMEs, corporates, and institutions that want a single partner instead of a scattered vendor list. You can engage one pillar, or we can coordinate the full stack — from company registration and brand identity through software, cybersecurity, and managed IT.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Mutag House do?

Mutag House is an end-to-end business solutions partner in Centurion, South Africa. We deliver business setup and compliance, marketing services, and technology services — software, cybersecurity, managed IT, cloud, and infrastructure — for startups, SMEs, corporates, and institutions.

Does Mutag House offer cybersecurity and managed IT?

Yes. Cybersecurity, managed IT, cloud, cabling, and custom ICT projects sit inside our Technology Services pillar, alongside custom software — and they can be combined with marketing and business setup when you need one partner.

Where is Mutag House located?

Mutag House is located in Centurion, Gauteng, South Africa, and serves clients across South Africa and internationally on a remote basis.

Can I work with Mutag House on just one service?

Yes. You can engage Mutag House for a single need — branding, a website, company registration, custom software, or managed IT — or for an end-to-end programme across business, marketing, and technology. Quotes are tailored after a consultation.

What industries does Mutag House serve?

Mutag House serves fintech, healthcare, logistics, retail, education, hospitality, and professional services — and adapts solutions to any organisation that needs to form, grow, or run the business.

Can Mutag House help with POPIA compliance?

Yes. Mutag House builds authentication and security systems with POPIA requirements in mind, covering user data handling, consent management, and access control.

Do you offer ongoing support after project delivery?

Yes. Mutag House provides long-term maintenance, performance monitoring, scaling support, and system updates after every project.

How do I get started with Mutag House?

Start with our onboarding form, schedule a consultation via the contact page, or reach out directly on WhatsApp or by email at info@mutag.co.za.
